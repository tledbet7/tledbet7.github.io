CRAP Website Evaluations

For this assignment, I chose two websites to evaluate based on the four CRAP design principles: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity. One website demonstrates good design, while the other shows areas where the design could be improved.

EXO Cosmetics

EXO Cosmetics is an example of a well-designed website. It has a neat and clean layout that makes it easy to navigate and find information.

  • Contrast: The EXO Cosmetics website uses white, grey, and pink throughout the website with black text. The colors work well together and make the information easy to read. The black font stands out against the lighter backgrounds, and the pink adds some color without making the website look too busy.
  • Repetition: The website is very consistent with its colors, fonts, and overall layout. The same white, grey, and pink color scheme is used throughout the different pages. This makes the website look organized and gives it a clean and professional appearance.
  • Alignment: The EXO Cosmetics website has a very neat and clean layout. The products, images, and text are organized in a way that makes everything look consistent. Nothing looks out of place, which makes the website easy to navigate.
  • Proximity: The website does a good job of keeping related information together. The product images, names, and prices are grouped together, making it easier for customers to find what they're looking for without the page feeling cluttered.

Arngren

Arngren is an example of a poorly designed website. The page has a cluttered layout with many different products, images, and text displayed together, making it difficult to navigate.

  • Contrast: The Arngren website has a lot of different colors, images, and text all over the page. There isn't much contrast between what is important and what isn't, making it difficult to know where to look first. Everything seems to compete for attention.
  • Repetition: The website doesn't have a consistent design throughout the page. The product images, text sizes, and spacing are all different, making everything look unorganized. There isn't a clear pattern that makes the website feel put together.
  • Alignment: The products, images, and text are placed all over the page without a clear layout. Some images are bigger than others, and the information doesn't line up evenly. This makes the website look cluttered and difficult to navigate.
  • Proximity: The website has so many products displayed close together that it's difficult to tell which information belongs to which product. There isn't enough spacing between the different sections, making everything feel crowded and overwhelming.
